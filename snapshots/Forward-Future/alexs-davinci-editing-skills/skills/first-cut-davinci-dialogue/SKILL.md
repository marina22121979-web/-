# First Cut Davinci Dialogue

1. Create a word-timed transcript and examine the full recording, retaining complete thoughts and the best takes.
2. Use source waveforms and transcript timing to remove false starts, repeated phrases, mistakes, and unnecessarily long pauses without clipping consonants or changing meaning.
3. Keep all synchronized camera, screen, and audio tracks aligned at each cut. Preserve the complete screen recording during a first pass.
4. Apply short centered audio fades where appropriate, inspect every cut edge and take, check sync and frame rates, and save a recoverable timeline.
