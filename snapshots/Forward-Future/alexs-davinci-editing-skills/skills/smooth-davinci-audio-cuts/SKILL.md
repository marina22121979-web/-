# Smooth Davinci Audio Cuts

1. Inspect existing dialogue cut points and preserve manual timing and linked video cuts.
2. Apply centered two-frame audio crossfades at eligible adjacent dialogue cuts unless another duration is requested. Avoid applying video transitions.
3. Check the beginning and end of each fade for clipped speech, clicks, doubled syllables, or exposed gaps. Preserve intentional pauses.
4. Configure an audio-only shortcut only if requested, and save and verify the timeline.
